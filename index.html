The brief

Keep organic social running for small-business clients across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, TikTok and YouTube. Each brand needed its own voice, calendar and approvals.

What I did

  • Planned monthly content calendars per brand in monday.com, mixing static posts, carousels and short videos.
  • Wrote detailed creative briefs for designers and video editors, so drafts arrived close to final.
  • Sent posts to clients through a review portal, handled revisions, then scheduled approved posts in OneUp.
  • Onboarded new clients with a three-post trial, delivered within three to five business days, so they could see the quality before committing.
  • Pulled monthly analytics reports across every platform to show what was working.

How I use AI

AI shortens the first draft. Judgement, editing and accuracy stay with me.

Research
ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude help me map a brand's audience, competitors and post angles quickly. I still read the brand's own channels before writing anything.
Drafting
First-pass captions and hooks, which I rewrite to fit the brand's voice.
Creative
AI-generated reels, UGC-style ad concepts and post visuals for testing ideas fast.
Reporting
Plain-language summaries of platform exports, with every figure checked against the source.

My rules: nothing goes to a client unedited, every number is checked against the platform report, and AI-made work is always labelled as AI-made.
